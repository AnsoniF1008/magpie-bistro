# Owner's guide — The Magpie Bistro website

Everything is done from the admin: **yourwebsite.com/admin**. It works on your phone. Sign in with your email and password.

The left menu (☰ on a phone) has three shortcuts at the top: **Orders board**, **This week's menu** and **View website**.

---

## Every day

### A dish ran out
1. Menu → **Dishes**.
2. Flip the **Sold out** switch next to the dish.

The website shows SOLD OUT right away and nobody can order it. Flip it back when you have more.

### Work the orders
1. Open **Orders board**.
2. Tap **Turn on new-order sound** once (leave this page open; it rings when an order is paid).
3. Each order is a card. Tap its button as you go:
   - **Ready ✓** when it is packed
   - **Picked up ✓** when the customer has it
   You can also drag a card to another column.
4. **Details** shows the ticket. From there you can **Print ticket** or **Cancel & refund** (owner only).

Columns: *Waiting for payment* (not paid yet — don't cook), *To prepare*, *Ready for pickup*, *Picked up today*.

### Close for the day
Settings → **Site settings** → Hours → tick **Closed today** and, if you like, write a message ("Closed for Thanksgiving — back Monday"). Save. Untick it when you reopen.

---

## Every week

### Set the weekly menu
1. Open **This week's menu**.
2. Tap a day to select it (it gets a gold border).
3. Tap dishes in the list to add them to that day. Tap **×** to remove one. On a computer you can drag instead.
4. Press **Save menu**. Nothing changes on the website until you save.

- **One week** changes only that week. Use ← → to prepare next week early. Days marked ROTATION are following the regular menu.
- **Regular rotation** is the menu shown every week unless you change a specific week.
- **Reset to rotation** throws away that week's changes.

A dish on the board can only be ordered for the day it is on. Dishes that are not on the board that week (bowls, cookies…) can be ordered any open day.

## Now and then

### Change a price, name or photo
1. Menu → **Dishes** → tap the dish.
2. Change what you need. Type prices like `15.00`.
3. To change the photo, tap the photo box and upload a new one. Describe the photo in a few words when asked — that description is read aloud to blind visitors.
4. **Save**.

Made a mistake? Open the dish → **Versions** → pick an earlier one → **Restore**.

### Add a new dish
Menu → **Dishes** → **Create New**. Give it a name, a price and a category, then Save. Add it to a day in **This week's menu** if it is a dish of the day.

- **Show on website** off = hidden everywhere.
- **Can be ordered online** off = shown, but customers ask at the counter.
- **Counter favorite** = featured with its photo.

### Custom cake and holiday requests
Customers send a request; nothing is charged until you approve it. You get an email for each one, and the dashboard shows how many are waiting.

1. Special orders → **Special requests** → open the request.
2. **To accept:** type the **Amount to charge now**, add a note if you want, set **Status** to *Approved* and Save. The customer is emailed a link to pay. When they pay, the status becomes *Paid* and you get an email.
3. **To decline:** write the **Reason**, set Status to *Rejected* and Save. The customer is emailed your reason.

For holiday orders the amount starts at the deposit; change it if you need to.

### Holiday pre-orders
Special orders → **Holiday campaigns**. Each holiday has an order-by date, a pickup day, a deposit percentage and its list of items with prices. The website shows a countdown and stops taking orders at the deadline. Untick **Show on the website** to hide one.

### Announcement banner
Settings → **Site settings** → Banner & photos → type the announcement. Leave it empty to hide it.

### Hours, tax and contact details
Settings → **Site settings**. Change the sales tax only after checking with your accountant.

### Add a helper
Settings → **Users** → Create New. Choose **Staff**: they can mark dishes sold out, work the orders board, set the weekly menu and answer requests, but cannot change prices, delete dishes or refund.

### See what changed and download your orders
- Settings → **Change history** lists who changed what and when.
- On the dashboard, choose two dates and press **Orders (CSV)** to open your orders in Excel or Google Sheets.

---

## If something looks wrong
- **An order was paid but is not on the board:** press ↻ Refresh. If it still is not there, look in Orders → Orders.
- **No sound:** tap the sound button again; phones mute pages that are in the background.
- **Locked out:** use "Forgot password?" on the sign-in page, or ask the owner account to reset it.
